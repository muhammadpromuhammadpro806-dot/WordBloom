How to Delete Your WordBloom Account

You can request deletion of your WordBloom account and all associated data at any time. Follow the steps below:

Method 1 — Delete Inside the App

Step 1: Open the WordBloom app on your device
Step 2: Tap your profile icon or go to Menu → Profile
Step 3: Scroll down and tap "Contact / Report"
Step 4: Type "Delete my account" and your registered email
Step 5: Submit — we will process your request within 7 days

Method 2 — Email Us Directly

What Data Gets Deleted

  • ✅ Your email address
  • ✅ Your username and profile
  • ✅ Your game progress, coins and level data
  • ✅ Your leaderboard entry
  • ✅ All messages sent through Contact/Report

Data Retention

Please note: After your account is deleted, all your game progress, coins, levels and achievements will be permanently lost and cannot be recovered.

We will complete your deletion request within 7 days of receiving it. Some anonymised data (not linked to your identity) may be kept for up to 30 days for security and fraud prevention purposes.
