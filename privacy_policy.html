Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 3, 2026
WordBloom is a free word puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and play WordBloom, we collect:

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account
  • Username — your display name in the game
  • Game progress — your level, coins, words found and achievements
  • Device information — device type and OS version for compatibility

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Create and manage your game account
  • Save your game progress across devices
  • Show your rank on the leaderboard
  • Enable multiplayer features
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Firebase and Data Storage

WordBloom uses Google Firebase to store account data and game progress securely. Firebase is a trusted Google service that follows strict security and privacy standards. Your data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and protected by Firebase security rules.

You can read Google Firebase's privacy policy at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Advertising

WordBloom may show advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information to show relevant ads. You can learn more about how Google uses data at: policies.google.com/privacy

5. In-App Purchases

WordBloom offers optional in-app coin purchases. All payments are processed securely. We do not store your payment information — all transactions are handled by the Google Play Store.

6. Children's Privacy

WordBloom is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Google Firebase — for data storage (as explained above)
  • Google AdMob — for advertising (as explained above)
  • When required by law

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Your account is protected by your password. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your WordBloom account.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Update your username or account information in the game settings

To delete your account or request your data, please contact us using the Contact option inside the game.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
